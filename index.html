FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Are you collection-only? When is delivery launching? ↓
We are currently **collection-only at The Galleria** while we finalize our official onboarding agreements and dispatch logistics with major delivery partners (UberEats, Deliveroo, and JustEat). We refuse to launch delivery until we are 100% certain our drivers can get our burgers from the griddle to your door while they are still steaming hot.

Want to know the exact day delivery drops in Hatfield? Enter your email or phone number in our VIP banner above and we will message you the second our delivery doors open!
Where exactly in The Galleria car park are you? ↓
Look for the matte-black food truck with the yellow lighting in the main public parking area near Comet Way. If you're driving in, park up, submit your order via our online checkout, and wait for your collection alert!
How long will my collection order take? ↓
Most orders are smashed, fried, and packed within **10 to 15 minutes**. When you submit your order at checkout, it routes directly to our kitchen staff via WhatsApp. We will reply instantly to confirm receipt and give you a precise pickup timestamp.
